Looking for a place to get screened?

Illinois · ages 45 to 75

Find screening near you

1. Community health centers

Federally funded health centers give care whether or not you have health insurance, and fees are on a sliding scale based on what you can pay. Ask about an at-home test kit or a colonoscopy referral.

Search by ZIP code (HRSA) ↗

2. Talk to someone, any time

The American Cancer Society helpline is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. They can help with questions about screening and cost.

1-800-227-2345

3. Already have a doctor?

Ask at your next visit: "Am I due for colorectal cancer screening? Is an at-home test right for me?"

Illinois is already trying this

The Illinois Department of Public Health funds statewide colorectal cancer outreach that includes community-based navigation. The 2026 to 2027 award went to the Illinois Public Health Association. The goal: 73.4% of adults 45 to 75 screened by 2027, on the way to the Healthy People 2030 target of 74.4%.

Source: IDPH Colorectal Cancer Screening program (CSFA 482-00-2942)
